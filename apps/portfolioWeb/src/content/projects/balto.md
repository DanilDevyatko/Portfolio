On this project, I developed cross-browser e-commerce stores on Shopify using the Liquid templating engine.

I focused on building reliable, maintainable storefronts that performed consistently across devices and browsers. Accessibility was treated as a core requirement, ensuring stores were usable for people with disabilities, not just visually polished.

Performance optimization was key - improving loading speed and eliminating unnecessary complexity to keep the experience fast and smooth.

I also worked directly with clients, translating business requirements into practical technical solutions and communicating trade-offs clearly.

This project strengthened my ability to combine technical precision with real-world business needs.
